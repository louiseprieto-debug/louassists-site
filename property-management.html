What I do

Two ways I look after your property

You are not on site. I am. I act as your coordinator and your eyes and ears, and I report back with photos and clear next steps.

01

Property oversight

  • Regular check visits: doors, shutters, leaks, humidity, alarm, mail and airing, with a photo report
  • Key holding and access for cleaners, gardeners, technicians and guests
  • Booking and supervising maintenance: pool, garden, boiler, air conditioning, repairs
  • Getting the property ready before you arrive, and closed up after you leave
  • Your local contact for the building manager (syndic), neighbours and emergencies
02

Renovation coordination

  • Finding artisans, requesting and comparing quotes
  • Planning the work and keeping a shared schedule
  • Site visits with photo and video reports
  • Following up on delays, budget and snags until handover

I coordinate on your behalf. I am not an architect or a project engineer (maître d'œuvre).

How it works

  • Monthly plans: 3 months minimum, then 1 month's notice. Paid in advance at the start of each month. Unused hours carry over for 1 month.
  • Visits and days: no commitment. 50% on booking and the balance on completion, or payment on completion for small jobs. Reply within 1 working day. Any other work is billed at €65 an hour, 2 hours minimum.

Extras

  • Extra hours beyond your plan: €65 an hour, agreed before use
  • Urgent work (same day, evenings, weekends): +25%
  • Travel: free within 15 km of Beausoleil. Beyond that, €0.60 per km, or a flat €25 per trip in the Monaco to Nice area
  • Expenses paid on your behalf are billed at cost, with receipts

Not included: rental management (collecting rents, managing leases), architectural design or acting as project engineer (maître d'œuvre), and legal or accounting advice.
